Privacy Policy for CorEasy App

Last updated: October 2026

CorEasy App is designed to help users manage and simplify their day-to-day core operations. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles user information.

1. Information We Collect

CorEasy App collects and processes certain information necessary to provide its core functions:

1.1 Personal Information

  • User name and contact details
  • Account identifier and login credentials for authentication

1.2 App Usage Data

  • Records and entries created within the app
  • Basic app activity needed to keep the service running and fix issues

All data is used strictly to provide and improve the functionality of CorEasy App and is not shared with third parties.

2. Permissions Used

2.1 Storage

Used to allow users to save and upload files that they choose to add within the app. Files are transmitted securely and are accessed only as needed for the feature being used.

2.2 Notifications

Used to send alerts related to reminders, updates, and other activity within the app.

3. Login and Authentication

The app requires users to log in using their account credentials. Authentication is handled securely and credentials are not shared with any third parties.

4. Data Sharing

CorEasy App does not sell, share, or transfer any user data to third parties. No external analytics or advertising services are used.

5. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can delete it.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable and appropriate security measures to protect user data, including secure transmission (HTTPS) and access controls to prevent unauthorized access.

7. Data Retention

User data is retained for as long as the account remains active or as needed to provide the service. Users may request deletion of their account and associated data by contacting us, and such data will be deleted in accordance with our data retention practices and applicable laws.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Updates will be posted on this page.

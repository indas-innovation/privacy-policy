Privacy Policy for Indus CRM App

Last updated: September 2026

Indus CRM App is designed to help sales and support teams manage customer relationships, including leads, contacts, deals, and follow-up activities. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles user information.

1. Information We Collect

Indus CRM App collects and processes certain information necessary for customer relationship management functions:

1.1 Personal Information

  • User name, designation, and department
  • Employee ID and contact details
  • Login credentials for authentication

1.2 Customer and Lead Data

  • Customer and lead names, contact numbers, email addresses, and company details
  • Deal, opportunity, and pipeline information
  • Notes, call logs, and follow-up activity records

1.3 Location Data

  • Location data used solely to verify field visits to customer or client sites
  • No location data is stored beyond what is required for visit verification

All data is used strictly for internal sales and customer relationship management purposes and is not shared with third parties.

2. Permissions Used

2.1 Location

Used to verify user presence at customer or client locations during field visits. Location is captured only at the time of check-in and is not tracked continuously.

2.2 Camera

Used for capturing photos during client visits, such as proof of visit or document images. Images are not shared externally and are stored securely within the organization's system.

2.3 Storage

Used to allow users to upload documents such as contracts, visiting cards, or profile photos. Files are transmitted securely and stored within the organization's system.

2.4 Contacts

Used, where permitted, to help users quickly add or match customer contacts. Contact data is not uploaded or shared without user action.

2.5 Notifications

Used to send alerts related to lead assignments, follow-up reminders, deal updates, and other CRM activities.

3. Login and Authentication

The app requires users to log in using credentials provided by the organization. Authentication is handled securely and credentials are not shared with any third parties.

4. Data Sharing

Indus CRM App does not sell, share, or transfer any customer or user data to third parties. All data is accessible only to authorized personnel within the organization. No external analytics or advertising services are used.

5. Children's Privacy

This app is intended for use by employees and authorized personnel within an organization and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable and appropriate security measures to protect data handled by the app, including secure transmission (HTTPS) and access controls to prevent unauthorized access.

7. Data Retention

Customer and user data is retained for as long as necessary to fulfill business and operational purposes or as required by applicable laws or organizational policies. Upon termination of employment or account access, data is handled in accordance with the organization's data retention policy.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Updates will be posted on this page.
